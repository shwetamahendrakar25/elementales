Sony Kurapati · Behind Elementales

Hey, I’m Sony — the Person Behind the Lens at Elementales.

For me, photography has always been about more than just taking pictures. It’s about understanding people, feeling the moment, and capturing emotions that words often can’t express.

With 7 years of experience in photography, 4 years in videography, and a passion for drone cinematography, I’ve learned that the most meaningful moments are often the ones that happen naturally, without a pose or a plan.

One thing that defines my approach is my ability to read people’s emotions even before I click the shutter. I pay attention to the little expressions, the quiet connections, and the feelings that make every person and every story unique. I don’t just want to capture how a moment looks — I want to capture how it feels.

Through Elementales, I bring together my creativity, perspective, and love for visual storytelling to turn fleeting moments into memories you can revisit for a lifetime.

Because the best photographs don’t just show a moment — they make you feel it all over again.
